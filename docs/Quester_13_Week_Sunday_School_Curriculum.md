# Quester 13-Week Sunday School Curriculum

Adapted from the existing Quester Community leader guide, Journey Journal structure, music/video themes, and the Identity Island digital quest materials in this project folder.

## Curriculum Aim

This 13-week Sunday school curriculum invites kids into the Quester world as a "Great Search for Truth." Each week treats a question as a quest, helping children discover that God's Word gives true answers to life's biggest questions and that the whole quest leads to knowing and following Jesus.

Assumed format: 50-60 minutes, mixed elementary or upper elementary. For younger kids, shorten discussion and lean harder on movement, object lessons, and drawing. For preteens, expand Bible lookup, journaling, and discussion.

## Quester Language to Keep

- Students are "Questers."
- Questions are quests that can lead us toward truth.
- The Bible is the trusted map for the quest.
- Paraclete is the Helper figure, connecting naturally to the Holy Spirit as Helper, Advocate, and Counselor.
- Quester Zest can be used for participation, kindness, memory verse effort, teamwork, and courage in asking honest questions.
- The five major destinations are Identity Island, Ocean of Origins, Mainland of Meaning, Morality Mountain Range, and Dominion of Destiny.

## Weekly Rhythm

1. Welcome and Quester Zest check-in: 5 minutes
2. Opening song or video clip: 5-8 minutes
3. Big Question and object lesson: 7 minutes
4. Bible discovery: 12-15 minutes
5. Quest activity, game, craft, or puzzle: 15-20 minutes
6. Remember-Me verse practice: 5 minutes
7. Closing prayer and take-home quest: 3 minutes

Use the full VBS video lessons when time allows. For normal Sunday school, use short clips, a song, or a recap from the leader.

## Weekly Lesson Sketches

### Week 1: The Quest Begins

Big Question: Why do questions matter?

Leader focus: Set the tone that Sunday school is a safe place to ask, seek, listen, and discover. Jesus Himself asked questions as a child, and God's Word gives light for the path.

Bible discovery: Luke 2:46 and Psalm 119:105.

Activity: Give each child a small "Quest Card." Ask them to write or draw one big question they have about God, life, themselves, or the Bible. Put the cards in a treasure box and tell them the class will keep questing together.

Remember-Me verse: Psalm 119:105.

Take-home quest: Ask someone at home, "What question helped you grow closer to God?"

### Week 2: Identity Island

Big Question: Who am I?

Leader focus: The Quester leader guide teaches that identity cannot be discovered by looking only inward. Kids need the light of knowing God to understand who they truly are.

Bible discovery: John 8:12 and John 1:12.

Object lesson: Use the leader guide's basketball idea in simplified form. A ball understands its purpose by the game it was made for. We understand ourselves by the God who made us.

Activity: "Who Am I?" cards. Kids ask yes/no questions to discover the identity taped to their back or held on their forehead.

Remember-Me verse: John 8:12.

### Week 3: Perception Pond

Big Question: Whose approval matters most?

Leader focus: The game's Perception Pond teaches that reflections can be misleading. Kids often measure identity by friends, popularity, appearance, performance, or feelings. God's truth is deeper.

Bible discovery: Galatians 1:10 and 1 Samuel 16:7.

Activity: Mirror notes. Put statements around a mirror: "popular," "smart," "funny," "athletic," "left out," "good enough?" Then cover them with truth cards: "known by God," "invited by Jesus," "made with purpose," "loved before I perform."

Quest connection: If possible, show or play the Perception Pond section from the Identity Island game.

Remember-Me verse: Galatians 1:10.

### Week 4: Cave of Confusion

Big Question: What do I do when I feel lost inside?

Leader focus: Confusion grows when we treat our own thoughts as the final source of truth. Jesus is the light who leads us.

Bible discovery: John 8:12 and Psalm 27:1.

Activity: Dark-room path. Create a short obstacle path with lights off or dimmed. One child tries it alone, then tries again with a flashlight and a guide. Debrief: light and guidance change the journey.

Quest connection: Use the "Cave of Confusion" song/video or game dialogue as a setup.

Remember-Me verse: John 8:12.

### Week 5: Palm Grove

Big Question: Who guides me when I am afraid?

Leader focus: The game uses Psalm 23 imagery to move kids from fear toward hope. This is a natural pastoral week for anxiety, loneliness, and trust.

Bible discovery: Psalm 23.

Activity: Shepherd's map. Kids draw a path with green pastures, still waters, a dark valley, a table, and home. At each stop, they write one way God cares for His people.

Quest connection: Use Palm Grove Poetry from the game if available.

Remember-Me verse: Psalm 23:1 or Psalm 23:4.

### Week 6: Truthful Treasure

Big Question: Where can I find truth about my value?

Leader focus: The Truthful Treasure game section names the problem clearly: the Quester cannot find identity by following the voice that says, "just follow your heart." The true path is following Jesus.

Bible discovery: Matthew 16:24-25 and John 14:6.

Activity: Treasure sort. Hide cards around the room. Some cards are "shiny lies" and some are "truth treasure." Kids sort them and match truth cards to Bible verses.

Remember-Me verse: John 14:6.

### Week 7: Ocean of Origins

Big Question: Where did everything come from?

Leader focus: God is Creator. Everything that exists depends on Him. Creation is not an accident, and people are not mistakes.

Bible discovery: Genesis 1:1 and Genesis 1:26-31.

Object lesson: Use the leader guide's building-block "Shake It to Make It" idea. Let kids see that design points beyond chaos.

Activity: Creation order relay. Teams put creation-day cards in order and name what each part shows about God.

Remember-Me verse: Genesis 1:1 or Genesis 1:27.

### Week 8: Image Bearers

Big Question: Why is every person valuable?

Leader focus: Humans are made in God's image. This gives dignity to every child, every neighbor, and every person who is different from us.

Bible discovery: Genesis 1:27 and Psalm 139:13-14.

Activity: Thumbprint art with magnifying glasses. Kids examine fingerprints and make a small artwork. Emphasize both sameness and uniqueness.

Discussion: What changes when I remember the person beside me is made by God?

Remember-Me verse: Genesis 1:27.

### Week 9: Mainland of Meaning

Big Question: Why am I here?

Leader focus: The leader guide frames purpose as knowing Someone, not merely doing something. We were made to know God and share His love.

Bible discovery: 1 John 4:7-8 and John 17:3.

Object lesson: Use the pitcher idea. A pitcher is designed to be filled and poured out. People are made to receive God's love and share it.

Activity: Meaningful matches. Match objects with what they were made for, ending with "people -> God" and "hearts -> love."

Remember-Me verse: 1 John 4:7-8.

### Week 10: Filled and Poured Out

Big Question: What does love do?

Leader focus: Love is not only a feeling. God's love becomes action, service, sacrifice, truth, and kindness.

Bible discovery: John 15:9-13 and 1 John 3:18.

Activity: Service quest planning. Let kids choose one small class service project: encouragement cards, snack bags, prayer cards, or helping reset a church space.

Song connection: "Beloved Be Loved."

Remember-Me verse: 1 John 3:18.

### Week 11: Morality Mountain

Big Question: How do I know right from wrong?

Leader focus: Morality comes from God's good character, not personal preference. God's commands show us what love looks like.

Bible discovery: Matthew 22:36-39 and selected Ten Commandments from Exodus 20.

Activity: Moral compass course. Give teams written directions to find destination markers. Then connect the idea: directions matter, and God's commands guide us toward love.

Song connection: "Morality: The Search."

Remember-Me verse: Matthew 22:37-39.

### Week 12: Grace at the Mountain

Big Question: What do I do when I have done wrong?

Leader focus: The guide's morality day turns toward grace. God's law shows our need, and Jesus gives forgiveness we cannot earn.

Bible discovery: Romans 3:23-24 and John 1:29.

Object lesson: Grace gift bag. Make clear that grace is not earned by being "best"; it is received as a gift.

Activity: Missing the mark. Toss beanbags at a target. Everyone misses at some point. Connect gently to sin, confession, forgiveness, and Jesus.

Remember-Me verse: Romans 3:23-24.

### Week 13: Dominion of Destiny

Big Question: What future does Jesus give?

Leader focus: The Quester VBS ends with destiny. Jesus' resurrection means death is not the final word for those who belong to Him.

Bible discovery: John 11:25 and 1 Peter 1:3-5.

Object lesson: Sunflower seed. A seed placed in the ground can become something more. Jesus gives resurrection hope.

Activity: Re-Quest review. Teams answer review questions from all five destinations. Award Quester Zest for truth, teamwork, and kindness.

Closing: Invite kids to name one truth they want to keep questing with. Give a simple gospel invitation and offer follow-up conversations with leaders and parents.

Remember-Me verse: John 11:25.

## Reusable Review Questions

- What is a Quester?
- Why are questions important?
- Where do we look for truth?
- What happens when we try to find identity only by looking inside ourselves?
- Who is the Light of the World?
- What does Paraclete mean?
- What does Genesis teach about where everything came from?
- What does it mean that people are made in God's image?
- What were people made for?
- How does God's love move through us to others?
- Who decides what is truly good?
- Why do we need grace?
- What future does Jesus promise His people?
